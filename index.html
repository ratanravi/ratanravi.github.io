Questions doctors ask

Do I pay before seeing the website?

No. You see your complete website first, ask for any changes, and pay only if you want it to go live.

How did you get my clinic's details?

From your public Google listing and patient reviews. Before going live, you check every detail and correct anything.

Who owns the website and web address?

You do. The web address is registered in your clinic's name, and you can take the website to anyone else later.

Can I change the photos, text or colours?

Yes. Send changes on WhatsApp and I update the site. You can see every change before paying.

Will patients still be able to book on WhatsApp if I'm busy?

Yes. Booking requests arrive as WhatsApp messages with the patient's name, concern and preferred time, so you or your staff can reply when free.

How do I pay?

By UPI or bank transfer, after you approve your website.

Got a website preview from me?

Reply in the same WhatsApp chat with any changes, or to make it live. I usually reply within a few hours.
