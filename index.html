How your website brings in patients

Patients search on their phone, check you out, and want to book without waiting on a call. Your website makes that one smooth path.

1They find you on Google

Your website gives Google your treatments, timings and location, so patients see more than just a map pin.

2They check you out and book

Treatments, doctors, timings and an "Open now" sign answer their questions. Booking takes about 20 seconds.

3The booking lands on your WhatsApp

No app to learn, no dashboard. You or your staff simply reply and confirm the time.

Questions doctors ask

Something else on your mind? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Do I really pay nothing before seeing it?

Yes. You see your complete website first, ask for changes, and pay only if you want it to go live. If you don't like it, you owe nothing.

Where did you get my clinic's details?

From your public Google listing and patient reviews. These can be out of date, so you check and correct every detail before anything goes live.

What if something on the preview is wrong?

Tell me on WhatsApp and I'll fix it on the same link. Previews are hidden from Google and marked as demos, so nothing incorrect is published.

Who owns the website and web address?

You do. The web address is registered in your clinic's name. Ask anytime and I'll send you all the website files.

Can I change things myself, or move to another developer?

Yes. Your website is plain, standard code that any web developer can edit. I'll hand over the files and help move the web address if you ever want to switch.

Do I need to pay for hosting every year?

No. Hosting is included with no yearly fee. From year two, you only renew your web address, usually ₹800–1,000 a year.

What if I want more features later?

Tell me what you need. I'll give you the price before starting, and you decide.

How will patient bookings reach me?

As WhatsApp messages with the patient's name, treatment and preferred time already filled in. You or your staff reply when free.
